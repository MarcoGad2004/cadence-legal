Cadence

Your day, in one place.

Cadence shows your Apple Calendar events next to your tasks, reminds you by time or place, and lets the people you plan with see your calendar. Private by design: we collect nothing, and there are no accounts. Your tasks stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and a calendar you share goes only to the people you invite, through your iCloud.

On iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac.
