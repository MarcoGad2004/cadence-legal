Privacy Policy

Cadence: Unified Day Planner · Last updated October 14, 2026

Cadence is designed to keep your schedule private. The short version: we collect nothing. Your tasks live on your devices and in your own iCloud, and your calendar is shared only with the people you choose.

What we collect

Nothing. Cadence has no accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics and no advertising, and it contains no third-party code. We, the developer, cannot see your tasks, events, name, photo or location. Everything described below happens on your devices, in your own Apple Account's iCloud, or in Apple's own services, never on a server we run.

Your calendar and reminders

With your permission, Cadence reads events from Apple Calendar on your device to show them next to your tasks, and creates or edits events when you ask it to. If you turn on the optional Apple Reminders sync, it also reads and writes the lists you choose in Reminders. This stays on your device and in the iCloud that Apple Calendar and Reminders already use.

Where your tasks live

Your tasks and lists are stored on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. If you use iCloud, Cadence keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. That copy is governed by Apple's iCloud terms, and the developer cannot access it.

Your name, photo and devices

The name and a small copy of the photo you set in Cadence are saved in your iCloud (its key-value storage) so your other devices can greet you the same way. Each of your devices also publishes a small status card to your iCloud: what kind of device it is, its name, the app version and which Cadence plan it has. Your devices use these cards to show each other, for example a Mac row on your iPhone. They stay in your own iCloud and the developer cannot see them.

Event countdown on more than one device

If you use the event countdown on both a Mac and an iPhone, the two devices agree on which one shows it. To do that, Cadence saves a short record for each upcoming event (its title, start and end, and which of your devices is showing the countdown) in your own private iCloud database. These records are ignored once the event is over or a day has passed.

Smart Add and Apple Intelligence

When you type a sentence to add a task or event (including with the Smart Add control), Cadence works out the day, time, place and list on your device. On devices that support it, this uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence through Apple's Foundation Models framework). The text you type is processed on your device and is not sent to us or to any server for this. On the Apple Watch, voice entry uses Apple's system dictation, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing your calendar

Sharing is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings. When you choose calendars to share and invite someone, Cadence copies those calendars' upcoming events (title, time, place, notes and calendar name), plus the name you set in Cadence, into a private area of your own iCloud account. This does move that information off your device and into your iCloud, and Apple's CloudKit then lets the people you invite read it. Only people you invite, and who accept, can read it, and they cannot change it. You can remove a person, stop sharing a calendar, or stop sharing with everyone at any time, which deletes the shared copy. Your tasks are never shared. The developer cannot see any of it. If someone shares a calendar with you, you see their events and name in Cadence, and only for as long as they keep sharing.

Location and travel alerts

Travel alerts are optional and part of Cadence Pro. When turned on, Cadence uses your device's location to ask Apple Maps how long it will take to reach an event's place, and sends a notification when it is time to leave. To keep the estimate accurate while the app is closed, travel alerts ask for "Always" location access. Your location and the destination are sent to Apple Maps for that estimate, under Apple's privacy policy. Cadence does not store your location history and never sends it to us. When an event has an address, Cadence shows it on a small map: the address is sent to Apple Maps to find its position, under Apple's privacy policy. If you choose Google Maps or Waze as your navigation app (a Pro feature), tapping an address opens that app with the address. Place reminders on tasks use your device's built-in region monitoring. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Cadence Pro and Cadence Circle purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your identity. Apple tells the app only which plan is active.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange your Cadence data directly with each other. Widgets, Live Activities, Spotlight search, Focus filters and Siri shortcuts run on your device.

Notifications

Reminders and travel alerts are scheduled on your device. No push notification server of ours is used. Apple's push service is used only to tell Cadence that a calendar shared with you has changed.

The Mac app

The Mac app works the same way: it reads Apple Calendar on your Mac, keeps your tasks on your Mac and in your own iCloud, and shows your iPhone's status through your iCloud. It adds no accounts and sends nothing to us.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no external fonts, scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

Deleting your data

You can delete tasks and lists in the app, stop sharing in Settings, and delete the app to remove its data from a device. Because Cadence syncs through your own iCloud, data kept there stays until you remove it: manage it in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage (choose Cadence and delete its data), or on a Mac in System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud. Events live in Apple Calendar and reminders in Apple Reminders, so deleting Cadence does not remove them.

Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may have the right to know what personal data a company holds about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale. We hold no personal data about you: we do not receive it, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete, and we never sell or share it. The data Cadence creates is under your control in the app and in your own iCloud (see Deleting your data above). If you would like to make a request anyway, or have a question, email us and we will point you to the right control or answer you.

Children

Cadence does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
