Why don't I see my calendar events?

Cadence needs permission to read Apple Calendar. Open iPhone Settings > Cadence > Calendars and choose Full Access. In Cadence, Settings > Calendars lets you show or hide each calendar.

Where is my data stored?

Tasks live on your devices and in your own iCloud, with no account to create. Your events stay in Apple Calendar. We, the developer, never receive any of it. If you share a calendar, a copy of those events is placed in your own iCloud for the people you invite to read.

Is there a Mac app?

Yes. The Mac app shows the same day, tasks and backlog, with a menu bar countdown for your next event. It uses the same iCloud, so your tasks match across devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete tasks and lists in the app, and stop sharing in Settings > Sharing (this removes the shared copy). Then delete the app from each device. Cadence's copy in iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage, by choosing Cadence and deleting its data. Events stay in Apple Calendar and reminders in Apple Reminders until you delete them there. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

What do Cadence Pro and Cadence Circle include?

Cadence Pro adds the full Apple Watch app (schedule with event details and a map, lists, task details, adding by voice and every complication; the Now page, the Tasks page and one complication are free), the Live Activity and Dynamic Island for your next event, travel alerts that tell you when to leave, and Smart Add, a control for your Action button or Control Center where you type anything and Cadence works out whether it's a task or an event, plus natural-language quick add. Smart Add works on devices that support Apple Intelligence, and runs on the device. Cadence Circle includes everything in Pro and adds sharing your own calendar and finding a time when you are all free. Seeing a calendar someone shares with you, widgets and Apple Reminders sync are free.

How do I share my calendar?

Settings > Sharing. Turn on the calendars people can see, then tap Invite Someone. They need Cadence and an iCloud account, and they must accept. You can share with up to 5 people. Sharing your own calendar needs Cadence Circle, but the people you invite can see it for free in the Shared tab. Your tasks are never shared.

How do I stop sharing or leave someone's calendar?

Settings > Sharing. Tap Remove next to a person, or Stop Sharing With Everyone. To stop seeing someone else's calendar, tap Leave next to their name.

How do travel alerts work?

For events with a place, Cadence asks Apple Maps how long the trip takes from where you are now and reminds you when to leave. Set location to Always in iPhone Settings > Cadence > Location so alerts stay accurate while the app is closed.

How do I restore or manage my plan?

In Cadence: Settings, tap the plan card at the top, then Restore purchases. To cancel or change a subscription: iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Cadence Circle can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Cadence Pro is personal. A couple needs to be in the same Apple family group for this to work: set one up in iPhone Settings > your name > Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all billing and refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

I have a code. How do I redeem it?

In Cadence: Settings, tap the plan card at the top, then Redeem a code.

How do I add the widgets or the Control Center button?

Widgets: long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search "Cadence". Control Center: open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control, and search "Cadence".
